# Ablauf – Confluence

Each branch is one of: long (positive), short (negative), neutral, or hollow.

- Long and short add to the tilt. The page shows that count as longs versus shorts.
- Neutral is a real reading with no tilt. The circle is gray.
- Hollow means there is no reading. The circle stays empty and the line says no data.
- A contradiction (the two 200 EMA methods disagree, or two oscillators point opposite ways) is marked and is not averaged into a side.

Sentiment, Fundamental, Sector, and Macro stay hollow until a sourced reading exists. Trend follows the 200 EMA. Momentum uses RSI 14 and the MACD histogram. Divergence and Structure use only swings and gaps that are present on the bars.
