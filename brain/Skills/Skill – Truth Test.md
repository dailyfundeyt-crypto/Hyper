# Skill – Truth Test

Never invent a number.

- An empty input, a failed exchange call, or a short series produces a hollow branch and the error or the reason.
- The 200 EMA is computed two ways. A mismatch is marked. It is not replaced with one of the two values.
- When two oscillators disagree, mark the contradiction. Do not average them into a softer side.
- The spoken script may count only branches that have a side. It says when a branch has no reading.
- The spoken script warns only. It states that no order was sent.
