# Skill – Trendfilter

The 200 EMA is the primary trend filter.

- Close above the 200 EMA: the trend branch is long.
- Close below the 200 EMA: the trend branch is short.
- Close equal to the 200 EMA: neutral.
- Also compute the 50 EMA and the 50 SMA and show them in the reason.
- If the 50 EMA disagrees with the 200 EMA, keep the 200 EMA side and say so. Do not average them.
- Compute the EMA a second way. If the two values disagree, the branch is a contradiction and has no side.
- Fewer than 200 closes: the branch stays hollow. Do not invent the EMA.
