# Skill – SMC Lite

Lite structure only. Elliott Wave stays manual (see `Memory – Elliott Wave`).

- Fair value gap: on three consecutive candles, a bullish gap is the third low above the first high. A bearish gap is the third high below the first low. The bounds are those candle prices.
- Swing high and swing low: a bar whose high or low is beyond both neighbors.
- Break of structure (BOS): the latest close breaks the last swing in the direction already printed by higher highs and higher lows, or by lower highs and lower lows.
- Change of character (ChoCh): the latest close breaks the last swing against that direction.
- If a gap and a break disagree, mark a contradiction.
- If there is no gap and no break, the branch stays hollow. Do not invent a level.
