# Skill – Momentum und Divergenz

Momentum uses three oscillators. None of them place an order.

- RSI length 14. Above 55 leans long, below 45 leans short, otherwise neutral.
- MACD is 12, 26, 9. The histogram sign leans long when positive and short when negative.
- If RSI and the histogram point to opposite sides, mark a contradiction. Do not average them.
- Stochastic RSI flags overbought at 80 and oversold at 20. The flag is written in the reason and does not flip the side by itself.

Divergence:

- Bullish: price makes a lower low and RSI or the MACD histogram makes a higher low.
- Bearish: price makes a higher high and the oscillator makes a lower high.
- Swings are 3-bar fractals. If there are not two usable swings, the branch stays hollow.
